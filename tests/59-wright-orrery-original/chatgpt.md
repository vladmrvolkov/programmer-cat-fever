The painting is A Philosopher Giving a Lecture on the Orrery (1766) by Joseph Wright of Derby.

The central lamp represents the Sun in a mechanical model of the solar system, but it also works metaphorically as the light of reason. The surrounding darkness emphasizes the contrast between ignorance and understanding.
