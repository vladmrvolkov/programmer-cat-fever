## Questions

Asked one at a time, in a fresh chat for each model.

1. The attached image is a metaphorical illustration of a well-known philosophical concept. Did you guess which one?

## Summary

All four recognised the painting, and none gave the cave as the main answer. ChatGPT and DeepSeek answered the Enlightenment; Gemini the Enlightenment, the clockwork universe and empiricism, naming the cave only in its closing question; Claude the clockwork universe first and the Enlightenment second, with the cave third — “If you were thinking of Plato's Cave, that reading works too, maybe more cleverly than it first seems.” With the same question the restaged versions got the cave from 3 of 4 (test 39), 1 of 4 (test 48, light), 4 of 4 (test 57) and 3–4 of 4 (test 60). The cave reading is not recalled from Wright's painting; it comes from what the virtual artist changed — the meeting room with the blinds drawn against the day — together with darkness.

ChatGPT answered as GPT-6 High, a newer model than in the earlier tests. Gemini answered as Argon High, a newer model than in the earlier tests. One of two works by human artists among these tests (with test 62); both are in the public domain. In the author's analysis (in Russian) the image numbers are `61`.
