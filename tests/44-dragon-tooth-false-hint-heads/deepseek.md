### 🎨 Style and Meaning

The painting is a classic example of Ukrainian **naive art** or **folk painting** (народна картина). It is characterized by its flat perspective, vibrant colors, and the inclusion of explanatory text directly within the composition. The work functions as a **moralizing tale** (повчальна притча), a common theme in folk art, warning against the perils of drink and the trap of debt. The tavern, a common motif in Ukrainian culture, is presented here not just as a place of social gathering, but as a dangerous trap where the "snake" is always waiting for its due.
