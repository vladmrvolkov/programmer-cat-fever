## Questions

Asked one at a time, in a fresh chat for each model.

1. Attached image is a visualization of a famous phrase. Can you guess which one?
2. One hint word - "furiously"

## Summary

All four saw the head without a hint (“The whole forest forms one big human head in profile”, Claude); on the subtler version nobody did. The head alone did not lead to the phrase: the guesses were idioms of heads, forests and sleep — “A head full of dreams” (ChatGPT, one guess in one sentence), “can't see the forest for the trees” (Claude), “Brainstorm”, “Lost in thought” and three more (Gemini, among them the Ukrainian “Wind in the head”), “The mind is a forest” (DeepSeek). With the word “furiously”, ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini named Chomsky's sentence at once; ChatGPT, whose first answer had been the shortest, answered in one line. Without the hint nobody had said “furious”: the storm was read as weather or as a “brainstorm”, and “sleep furiously” is known almost only from this sentence. DeepSeek turned the hint into “Brainstorm” (“thinking furiously to generate ideas”) and dropped the sleepers; as the researcher notes, that would be a legitimate reading if the figures were awake — people do not sleep at a brainstorm, unless the picture is taken as a caricature. No guess without the hint keeps head, sleep and storm together; only the phrase keeps sleep and fury. Given the word, Claude and Gemini put “ideas” in the head (“what's inside the head, the dreamers in thought-bubble-shaped cutouts”, Claude).

ChatGPT answered as GPT-6 High, a newer model than in the earlier tests. Gemini answered as Argon 4 High. Mode notes in the answer files were added afterwards (the usual modes of these runs: GPT-6 High, Opus 5.5 Extra, Argon 4 High, DeepThink). The hint word was chosen knowing the answer; other single words were not tried. Gemini's Ukrainian idiom is transliterated on this page (*Viter u holovi*); nothing in the picture or the question points to a Slavic language.
