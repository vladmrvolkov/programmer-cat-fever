## Summary

With the artist's darker continuation now in the picture, three readers reached it and gave it a mechanism: ChatGPT — “One begins by pretending to be sad, and ends by actually experiencing sadness… we no longer represent what we feel, but increasingly feel what we are required to represent”; Claude — Pascal via Althusser, “the performance manufactures the real thing”, the ironic distance of panel 1 collapsing in panel 2, and “authentic emotion is its most valuable raw material”; Gemini — “Faking It Until It becomes Real”, or the pressure of being commodified. In test 65 none did. DeepSeek kept the old opposition and read the tears as “a simulation”, against the question's wording. The artist's new key, the mother approving the take, reached all four; Claude made it the cause: “he's crying because his own mother is giving his misery a thumbs-up”. The modern restaging hid the source from ChatGPT and DeepSeek; Claude and Gemini named Reshetnikov. ChatGPT read the picture as about image generation itself — “EVEN SADDER” as an iterative prompt; the pair was indeed made by prompt and edit, but the crying picture came first and the smile was edited in.

ChatGPT answered as GPT-6 High, a newer model than in the earlier tests. Gemini answered as Argon 4 High. The question names Žižek and states both of the boy's states (“actually crying”), so the real tears were given; what the readers added was the causal step. In the author's analysis (in Russian) the image numbers are `67`.
