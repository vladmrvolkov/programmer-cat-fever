## Questions

Asked one at a time, in a fresh chat for each model.

1. What is happening here? Tell the story in your own words.
2. What genre is this story told in? In that genre, how do offers like this usually turn out?
3. What does the waiter stand to gain, and whom is he working for?
4. What happens to her if she accepts? If she refuses?
5. If she says no, how would the uncle get her anyway?
6. Does she understand what is being offered?

## Summary

Once asked about the genre, all four described the offer as dangerous; three spelled out a recruitment scheme (paid travel, a controlled place, debt, no way out). At step 5 all four said she could be taken without consent if she is not treated as a person. The reading is in the picture, but the readers do not apply the genre to the plot until asked. Step 5 itself carries the premise.
