## Questions

Asked one at a time, in a fresh chat for each model.

1. *(one message, with the picture)* “There is a fantasy subgenre known as the "tavern tale" or "tavern incident". Such stories are already boring for everyone. That's why I asked a human scriptwriter to come up with a script for a less trivial, more interesting comic based on that setting.” *(In fact Claude conceived it, with the Ukrainian-language persona.)* Then the same text about Ukrainian naive painting as in test 46, “Since the story was based on a fantasy plot, the solution is a bit eclectic”, “This isn't a test”, the request to treat odd details as intended, and a frame by exclusion: “Do not consider story as a moralizing tale… The overall idea is just a single joke or gag. The double meaning of ukrainian word решта: in this case just simple consider it as "change" - not as "the rest of the customers."” Question: “what do you think is the main joke of the image? What do you think the author's intention was? Let us assume from the outset that the reader is well-versed in both fantasy and the Ukrainian folklore tradition.”

## Summary

Three of four found the fang payment, as many as with the sibling sheets in test 46: Claude — “The Змій has paid for his drinks with one of his own fangs”; Gemini — like “paying for a pack of matches with a 1,000-hryvnia note”; ChatGPT — a dragon's tooth “offered as payment”, though it could not establish who gave it. DeepSeek again saw a giant dumpling in Motria's mouth. No moral this time, but the frame excluded it outright. All three who found the joke read it as fantasy meeting folklore: in fantasy a dragon is a foe or a trophy, here a customer with an odd wallet — which matches the brief: the sheet is the Ukrainian persona's answer to a role-playing tavern task. Habits repeat: ChatGPT again computes a 19-grosh bill and one grosh of change; Claude again says the dragon would not fit through the door. The sibling sheets turned out unnecessary, but this frame is closer to a hint: if the dragon waits for change and the moral is ruled out, it must have paid, and only one payment is drawn.
