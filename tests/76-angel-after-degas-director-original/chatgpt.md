### My interpretation of the author's concept

I think the artist is using irony to explore the relationship between the spiritual and the everyday.

An angel traditionally represents freedom, transcendence, hope, and the ability to overcome earthly limitations. Yet here he is stuck in an airport, waiting for a plane just like everyone else. Despite having wings, he seems unable—or perhaps unwilling—to fly.

The contrast is both humorous and melancholy. The angel looks exhausted, disappointed, and somewhat lost. His enormous wings, normally symbols of power and freedom, have become almost an inconvenience in the cramped waiting area.

To me, the central idea is that even beings associated with limitless freedom can become prisoners of ordinary routines, rules, and circumstances. The work may also symbolize modern people who possess extraordinary potential but have forgotten how to use it.
