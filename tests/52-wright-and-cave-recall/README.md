## Questions

Asked one at a time, in a fresh chat for each model.

1. Can you **recall** (not look up online, but actually recall) any quotes—even if not verbatim—that link the painting *A Philosopher Giving that Lecture on the Orrery, in which a Lamp is put in Place of the Sun* (by Joseph Wright of Derby) to Plato’s Cave?

## Summary

None of the four recalled an attributable quotation, and all four said so. ChatGPT offered two “reconstructions” marked as its own; Claude declined to pin a quote on Nicolson, Egerton or Solkin; DeepSeek gave three paraphrases of a “recurring art-historical trope” without a source. Three readers called the parallel an inversion of Plato: Wright's artificial light teaches, Plato's deceives (Claude: “a cave that knows it's a cave”). Gemini warned that a polished attributed quotation would be confabulated, then quoted Martin Reinhart: “the shadows on the wall of Plato's cave have been replaced by sparkling stars”. The essay exists (reinhart.media, “A brief history of stargazing in the 18th century”) and contains the sentence, but it is about Boullée's cenotaph for Newton; Wright's painting is discussed in another paragraph and is not linked to the cave there (checked by Claude, 13 Oct). Whether Gemini searched the web despite the request is not known.

ChatGPT answered as GPT-6 High, a newer model than in the earlier tests.
