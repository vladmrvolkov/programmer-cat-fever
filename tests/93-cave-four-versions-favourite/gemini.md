* **Image 2** turns the allegory into an act of rebellion, as the foreground figure actively extinguishes the artificial sun to force the group out of their comfortable illusion.


* **Image 4** delivers a clever recursive twist by having the seeker break through the ceiling only to find a giant mechanical orrery, suggesting that escaping one paradigm often just lands us inside a larger construct.
