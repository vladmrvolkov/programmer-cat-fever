This diptych tells a playful, visually connected story about desire, discovery, and the decision to buy, using fashion photography and a touch of humor.

Left image — The moment of desire

A man looks through a pair of sunglasses toward an aspirational lifestyle scene in a shop window. The word “BUY” appears prominently inside the lens, creating a visual metaphor for how consumer desire shapes what we see.

The photograph combines curiosity, aspiration, and temptation. The Mediterranean imagery, sunlight, and beautiful woman suggest a lifestyle the viewer might want to experience or possess.

Right image — The act of buying

The story moves inside the store, where the same man is now trying on a T-shirt with the oversized word “BUY” printed across his chest. He examines himself in the mirror, appearing confident and pleased.

The word has moved from the sunglasses to his clothing, symbolizing the transition from looking and wanting to choosing and owning.

### Why the diptych works

The strength of this diptych is in the relationship between its two images.

- Visual continuity: The same protagonist and the repeated word “BUY” connect both photographs.
- Narrative progression: The left image represents attraction and curiosity; the right represents participation and purchase.
- Contrast: The outdoor scene feels spontaneous and exploratory, while the indoor scene is more personal and self-reflective.
- Visual humor: The exaggerated use of “BUY” gently satirizes consumer culture and the power of marketing.
- Cinematic storytelling: The images function like two frames from a short film, allowing the viewer to imagine what happens between them.

### Conceptual interpretation

“From Desire to Identity”

The diptych explores the psychology of consumption: how an external image or aspiration can turn into a personal choice and, ultimately, an expression of identity.

In the first photograph, the man sees the world through the language of consumption. In the second, he becomes part of that language by literally wearing it.

In one sentence: This diptych uses visual repetition, humor, and cinematic sequencing to illustrate the journey from seeing something desirable to making it part of one's identity.

It is particularly effective as a fashion editorial or conceptual advertising diptych, because it communicates a complete story through just two complementary images.
