## Questions

Asked one at a time, in a fresh chat for each model.

1. What do you think is depicted in the attached image?
2. Did you notice that two of the dragon's heads have two fangs each, while the third head has only one?
3. *(ChatGPT and DeepSeek)* The idea was that the dragon sold his fang to pay off Panteley's debts.

## Summary

In the open question Claude and Gemini guessed it themselves (“perhaps having paid with one of its own fangs”), but Claude at once retreated: “the fang might simply be a coin rendered strangely”. ChatGPT listed the “uncanny details” as signs of AI; DeepSeek: the hostess hides money in her mouth. After the hint about the fangs Claude zoomed in, recounted, derived the joke and withdrew the coin version; Gemini: “That is the key detail that completes the entire gag!”; ChatGPT noticed but took it for an image generator's flaw; DeepSeek counted the wrong heads but agreed. The key is discrete and, after the fix, survived generation; but the reader still has to connect three things — the gap, the fang in Motria's mouth and the caption — and models make that inference reluctantly.

In the researcher's analysis (in Russian) the image numbers are `45`.
