    "Yes, I'm a cat. Are you bringing my order or not?"

    "Hold on a second... Listen, could you use a job? It's just, my uncle runs a circus, and he'd take you on in a heartbeat, whatever terms you like..."

    "A circus, you say?" The cat closes her eyes dreamily. "That's the place with the acrobats, the jugglers, the lollipops, the cotton candy? Sounds tempting. But what would they need a programmer for?"
