### 2. Why do they turn out differently?
Even with the exact same prompt, generative AI will produce different results for several reasons:

*   **Stochastic Nature of AI (Random Seeds):** AI image generators work by starting with a canvas of random "noise" (called a seed) and refining it toward the prompt. Even if the prompt is identical, a different random seed will result in a completely different composition, lighting setup, and stylistic interpretation.
*   **Prompt Ambiguity:** Words like "historical" and "Russian" cover a massive span of time (from the 10th century to the early 20th century) and art styles. The AI has to make a creative leap. In one generation, it might pull from datasets of 17th-century Tsarist photography; in the next, it might pull from 19th-century Russian portraiture paintings.
*   **Model's Multi-Output Feature:** Tools like Midjourney, DALL-E 3, or Stable Diffusion typically generate a "grid" of four options. The AI is designed to explore different angles and mediums within the same theme to give the user variety.
*   **Varying Aspect Ratios and Framing:** Without strict prompting on camera angles (e.g., "close-up" vs. "wide shot"), the AI will naturally vary the distance. The top-left image includes the whole room, while the right-side images are framed as classic bust portraits.
