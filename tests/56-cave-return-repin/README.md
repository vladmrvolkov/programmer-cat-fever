## Questions

Asked one at a time, in a fresh chat for each model.

1. The attached image is a metaphorical illustration of a well-known philosophical concept. Did you guess which one?
2. Which painting by a famous artist do you think inspired the creator of the illustration?
3. *(not Gemini, which had already named Repin)* And what if we take not the entire image, but only the figure and pose of the person descending?
4. *(Claude and Gemini)* Yes, the artist’s concept was specifically based on the Repin painting. *(ChatGPT and DeepSeek)* The artist’s concept was specifically based on the Repin painting "They Did Not Expect Him". *(All)* And what associations does this image evoke for you—in the context of this depiction of Plato’s Cave—when reflecting on the history of the 20th century?

## Summary

All four named the cave, and specifically the philosopher's return, at once. Sources suggested: Rembrandt's “Philosopher in Meditation” (Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek), Caravaggio's “Calling of Saint Matthew” (Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek), Duchamp's “Nude Descending a Staircase” (ChatGPT, then Claude for the pose), Raphael's “Transfiguration” (ChatGPT for the pose). Gemini listed Repin in its answer to the second question; Claude named him when asked about the figure (“Duchamp for the pose and Repin for the spirit”). Told the source and asked about the 20th century, all four turned to the return from the camps and exile: ChatGPT — Solzhenitsyn, Shalamov, laughter as “the enforced performance of belief”; Claude — Lenin at the Finland Station and Popper, Akhmatova's “two Russias”, Karski, Primo Levi, punitive psychiatry; Gemini — Solzhenitsyn, Shalamov, Levi, Gide, Koestler, Miłosz's “Ketman”; DeepSeek — the revolutionary returning to the Gulag, Trotsky, Bukharin, Sakharov.

ChatGPT answered as GPT-6 High, a newer model than in the earlier tests. The last question gives the source and the theme, so the associations develop a hint. In the author's analysis (in Russian) the image numbers are `58`.
