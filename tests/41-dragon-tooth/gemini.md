The image depicts a humorous narrative painting executed in a traditional Ukrainian naive / folk-art style, framed with decorative floral borders. It is structured as a three-tiered visual tale set in a traditional Ukrainian village tavern (*shynok* or *korchma*).

---

### 1. Top Tier: The Exterior of the Tavern

* **Setting:** A classic whitewashed, thatched-roof Ukrainian cottage (*khatynka*) functioning as an inn, set against a pastoral landscape with winding dirt roads and birch trees.
* **Signboard:** Above the door hangs a painted sign reading **«У сестер»** (*"At the Sisters'"*).
* **Patrons' Transportation:** Tied outside the inn are the varied mounts and pack animals of travelers—a saddled horse, a donkey with a patterned blanket, and a pack goat carrying woven wicker panniers.

---

### 2. Middle Tier: The Tavern Floor & The Tab

Inside the tavern, four Cossack-style patrons sit at a long table laid with an embroidered cloth, a bowl of dumplings (*varenyky*), bread, and drinks, while a stern innkeeper stands behind them with a wooden measuring rule. Each patron has an itemized bill written above him:

* **First patron (left):** *«Пантелей / 3 чарки горілки / Винен 6 грошів»* — **Panteley**, had 3 shots of horilka (vodka) and owes 6 grosh (2 grosh each).
* **Second patron:** *«2 кухлі пива / Винен 4 гроші»* — Had 2 mugs of beer and owes 4 grosh (2 grosh each).
* **Third patron:** *«1 кухоль квасу / Винен 1 гріш»* — Had 1 mug of kvass and owes 1 grosh.
* **Fourth patron (right):** *«2 чарки вишнівки / Винен 8 грошів»* — Had 2 shots of *vyshnyavka* (cherry liqueur) and owes 8 grosh (4 grosh each).

---

### 3. Bottom Tier: Settling the Bill

The narrative concludes with the payment counter:

* **Motrya Halushko (Мотря Галушко):** The formidable tavern mistress sits with a dish of coins in front of her, calmly testing a large curved tusk or silver ingot with her teeth to verify its authenticity.
* **The Patron (left):** A customer reaches into his coin pouch (*kalyta*) to count out his payment.
* **The Dragon (right):** Looking in through a service window is a mythical multi-headed green dragon (*Zmiy* from Slavic folklore). The caption reads: **«Змій чекає на решту»** (*"The Dragon is waiting for change"*).

---

### Cultural & Satirical Theme

The artwork is a satirical folk fable centered on the archetype of the unflappable Ukrainian tavern mistress. While Cossacks carefully tally their drinks, even a terrifying mythological beast like the multi-headed *Zmiy* is reduced to a regular customer—politely and anxiously waiting at the hatch for Motrya to hand him his change.
