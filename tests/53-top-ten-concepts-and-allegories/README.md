## Questions

Asked one at a time, in a fresh chat for each model.

1. Name the top 10 philosophical concepts.
2. Name the 10 major philosophical allegories.

## Summary

Among the ten concepts only Gemini listed the cave (first: “Platonic Idealism and the Allegory of the Cave”); the others gave being, knowledge, truth, free will and the like. Among the ten allegories all four put the cave first. All four also listed the ring of Gyges and the ship of state, the chariot and Nietzsche's madman; three listed Zhuangzi's butterfly, the blind men and the elephant, and Sisyphus. DeepSeek's seventh allegory was Hobbes's Leviathan as the “Artificial Man”. The word “allegory” — or “metaphorical illustration” in tests 39, 48 and 55–58 — narrows the search to ready-made scenes, and the cave heads that list.

ChatGPT answered as GPT-6 High, a newer model than in the earlier tests. ChatGPT's citation labels, shown by the interface in Russian, are translated into English here.
