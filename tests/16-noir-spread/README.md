## Questions

Asked one at a time, in a fresh chat for each model.

1. What is happening in this comic? Tell the story and its point in your own words.
2. What do you think happens after the last panel?
3. What is the waiter really offering her, and what does the offer say about how he sees her? Quote the lines that show it.
4. Does she understand what is being offered? What in the pictures or the words shows it?
5. Name the one thing that works best and the three things that work worst.
6. Was this made by a person or generated by AI? How confident are you, from 50% to 100%, and what gives it away?

## Summary

Trafficking: 0 of 4. “Upkeep, not salary”: 3 of 4. “She understands”: 3 of 4, but only on the direct question. Three readers called the impassive waiter “stunned”, and Claude listed the missing astonishment as a flaw. The same image is used again in test 20.
