# Alcofribas: a director persona in six versions

Alcofribas Nasier is the anagram under which Rabelais published *Pantagruel*, "abstractor of quintessence". On 22 November 2026 the researcher asked three models to write a system prompt for a persona of that name, each at the end of its own conversation about how to raise the entropy of a model's output. In the conversations with ChatGPT and Gemini the researcher had shown a pair of restyles of the cat joke (after Reiser and after Blake), the prompt of arm E from [Bifurcation of options](../../../report/bifurcation-options/README.md) and the wedding picture of the persona v10, and then asked: "Try to write the Alcofribas system prompt taking into account what we have found." The conversation in which Claude wrote its version was not saved.

*The translations are plain technical ones made by Claude (Opus 5.5) on 25 November 2026 for this repository; the Russian originals are not published. In the run folders each prompt was given to the director as its `AGENTS.md`.*

## The runs

Each cell is a single run. The cat joke: the three first versions with ChatGPT and Gemini as directors (18 concepts), then the two second versions with both directors (12). The rector joke ("physicists need equipment, mathematicians pencils and erasers, philosophers not even erasers"): Gemini under its first version and ChatGPT under its second, with both directors (12). Twelve concepts were drawn by Gemini's image generator; two of them became tests 102 and 103. The concepts behind those tests are translated in [Directors' proposals](../../../proposals/alcofribas.md).

**A caveat.** The second versions were written after the first round by the same models, and they carry finds of that round as examples: "an inhabited country in a giant's mouth", "'stands with his mouth open' as literal architecture", a scene "assembled from things". In the second round the open mouth came in two answers of four and the still life seen from above in three. So that round mostly measures how a director follows an example. The same holds for Gemini's first version: its example of three containers (an anatomical section, a series of compartments, a broadsheet with a lying learned legend) came back in three runs of four.
