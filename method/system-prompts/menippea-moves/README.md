# Menippea as moves: a director without a persona

*Written in Russian by Claude on 23 November 2026 at the researcher's request; the translation is a plain technical one made by Claude (Opus 5.5) on 25 November 2026. In the run folders the prompt was the director's `CLAUDE.md` (`AGENTS.md` for Codex); here it is [director.md](director.md). The Russian original is not published.*

The researcher asked for the Alcofribas prompt [claude-v2](../alcofribas/claude-v2.md) without Alcofribas and Rabelais, and for Menippean satire to be taken not as requirements but as the director's moves. In v7 the features of the menippea, after Bakhtin, were properties of the finished picture: the director picked one to three of them to fit a reading already chosen. Here they are operations on the skeleton of the source, and dice choose them before any reading.

**What it keeps from claude-v2:** the skeleton of the source and margin notes on the characters; the council of six outsiders (formerly Panurge's council); dice counted from the letters of the source's words (formerly Bridoye's dice); the lines re-voiced forward, beat by beat; no ranking; inconsistencies left unfixed; the craft and the limits.

**What is new:** two fixed lists of six moves, so that each candidate gets four dice — the council, a move of "world and frame", a move of "people and speech", and the voice:

| Move | Feature of the menippea (Bakhtin), as the project used it |
|---|---|
| B1 Down | the fantastic with "slum naturalism": the place worn out, the impossible untouched |
| B2 Threshold | dialogues on the threshold, "ultimate questions", answered practically |
| B3 Second plane | three planes and the mixing of styles: a second world in another manner |
| B4 Outside viewpoint | experimental fantasy: a point where nobody in the source stands |
| B5 Inserted document | inserted genres: a document with a compiler and an interest of its own |
| B6 Utopia and its price | the social utopia |
| C1 Scandal | scandals and breaches of decorum |
| C2 Mésalliance | oxymoron and mésalliance |
| C3 Crowning and decrowning | carnival crowning and decrowning |
| C4 Splitting | unusual states of mind; a line against the hands |
| C5 Two confident readings | the fantastic testing an idea |
| C6 A phrase done by hand | not a feature of the menippea: a turn of phrase carried out literally |

Laughter became a working question of step 4 ("who finds it funny now, and at what?"), open-endedness the rule "the moral — to nobody", and topicality was left out as a move: in the project it pulled pictures towards agitprop.

**After the first run (23 Nov)** the dice rule was corrected: within one list a number does not repeat, and a rethrow uses the next words of the source. The same source still gives every model the same numbers; this was kept on purpose, so that the directors can be compared on the same cells.

**Used by** Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini as directors on the cat joke and the rector joke (23 Nov), and on a request to turn two of the researcher's diagrams into a series. Gemini's third candidate on the cat joke — a veterinary dietitian as the outsider, the move "down", a mésalliance and the voice of the Soviet magazine *Krokodil* — became test [104](../../../tests/104-buffet-vet-idea-source-objection/README.md); the three candidates are translated in [Directors' proposals](../../../proposals/cat-joke.md), section 5. What the runs showed is in [Bifurcation of options](../../../report/bifurcation-options/README.md), section 11.
