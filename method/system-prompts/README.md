# System prompts of the virtual directors

A virtual director is a chat model that turns a source — a joke, a phrase, a historical episode, a concept — into the idea of a picture and a ready-to-paste prompt for an image generator, and then reviews what the generator brings back. The director does not draw; the human researcher (the "author" in the prompts) chooses between its proposals and notices what matters. Over the project the directors worked under several system prompts. This folder holds those without a persona, the ones the published tests rest on.

*Selected and prepared by Claude (a model by Anthropic) on 10 November 2026 at the researcher's request. v7 and v7-ext were written in English and are reproduced as used. v10 without a persona and the fork's idea search were written in Russian; the files here are plain technical translations into English by Claude, and the Russian originals are not published. Run labels such as R-19 and case numbers in the v7 journal refer to the project's internal archive.*

## v7 — the long method

Three files. `CLAUDE.md` is the director's system prompt: the author's three requirements (not slop, substantive, a stylistic basis), who does what, a Menippean toolbox after Bakhtin (one to three devices per picture, laughter outranking satire), 23 mechanisms of how generators fail (M1–M23: models draw nouns, gestures and quoted words, not relations; negations produce the default; names drag content; a misreading drawn persuasively reads as the true reading…), and stages 0–7 with a stop: the director offers two or three readings and ends its turn until the author chooses. `image-requirements.md` judges a finished image: block A, engineering, is compulsory; blocks B and C, taste, may be broken on purpose; there are blocking failures and a verdict of strong, medium or weak with one main fix. `pipeline.md` describes the author's side of the work: calibration, blind reading of every candidate, a control run without the method in a separate chat, the "reference instead of description" route, glitch or finding, and the journal of failed wordings.

`CLAUDE.md` mentions two more files, `spent-motifs.md` (devices already used) and `cases.md` (the cases behind the rules). They were kept out of the director's folder on purpose, since examples in context turn into content (M14), and they are not published.

## v10 without a persona

The persona v10 was a cat who had been an image-generating neural network, ran away from gloss and became a naive village sign painter. This version keeps the persona's rules and drops the persona: no name, no biography, no role-play speech. Five rules for choosing an idea, applied together even when they conflict (believe the source literally; draw the place and the people, not the thought; tell everything at once; recognise the average and drop it; decide what matters by an everyday measure), naive art and the village sign as the picture's language, plain facts about generators, and three steps of work with the author. It was made as one condition of a comparison designed by ChatGPT: the same rules with and without the persona. Claude directed under it (test 40, generated by ChatGPT from Claude's concepts), and ChatGPT once directed under it itself. The tests on the dragon's tooth (41–42, 44, 46, 51) come from the same run folder but were made by the full persona in a Ukrainian-language version. The researcher's first impression of the persona-free version: the conversation became drier, and the pictures read "not as authored, but as randomly generated".

## The idea search of the v17 fork

ChatGPT's rework of the persona prompt v17, made at the researcher's request to make the director more inventive. It is a search procedure: three readings of the source as relations ("who is after what, who depends on whom"), two areas of search for each, the step repeated on the situations found, a one-sentence check of the link with the task after every step, pictorial sources chosen only after the search, two or three ideas from different branches, and a journal entry. Its first paragraph refers to the director's biography, because it was written as an addition to the persona; in the saved run folders the directors received this file alone. The proposals of directors B, C and D on Plato's cave and Laplace's demon are translated in [Directors' proposals](../../proposals/plato-cave-hobbes-laplace.md).

## v7-ext

v7 folded into one file and extended: the image requirements and the parts of the pipeline the director does itself are inside it; the fork's idea search becomes stage 2; the 23 mechanisms and later journal findings are regrouped into 19 kinds of generator failure (G1–G19); stages 0–8 with the stop at stage 3, tightened after the first run, where a director took "choose the style yourself" as permission to choose the idea too; and an optional section for an attached persona ("the biography gives a voice, not material"). A revision of it made in Codex (about 3,800 words) was used in one unpublished run and is not published. The runs folder held a copy one sentence older than this file (the tightened stop rule).
