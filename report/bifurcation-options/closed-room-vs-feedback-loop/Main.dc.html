1Closed room. What models produce alone, without the loop — the union of three model families over arms A–C and F (D and E were marked by axes, not items; F used newer Claude and Gemini). One model covers 8–18 of 50 directors’ items; three in one arm, 18–25; all arms, 28. Union: 41 of 69 author-list items. It also finds things the loop did not (top right).
2Defaults. Fillers for what the joke leaves unspecified; displaced by specifying. The five generator defaults on the author’s list all came in arm A.
3Head attractors. Shared by the three models: recurrent fillers of a slot — readings and objects (laptop, </>, poster). Prohibition does not remove one; another occupant of the slot does. Under VS, Claude repeated a shared move — “the circus needs her work” (all three models in arm A; many readers before) — in 17 of 30 (plain list: 4–5).
4Own and tail attractors. Gemini: the dream bubble changes medium. ChatGPT, asked for a social subtext: “valued for what she is, not for what she can do” — seen in the loop too. All three: sweeping breed and era (styles came from the prompt's list).
5Feedback loop. Models, readers and a curator: 28 of 69 author-list items lie outside the closed-room union (arm A alone: 32).
6Producers and selector. Producers: directors — models working with the curator (22 of 28); generator glitches read by model readers (“no cat”); readers of a new picture (bakeneko). Selector: the curator. Glitch + reader is a mutation; without an independent selector the same pareidolia is confabulation.
7The loop’s own attractors. One-ink printed satire (the method); ivory paper with one vermilion (ChatGPT as director).
8Avoided unless named. The hard reading — the cat as an outsider. 0 of 24 closed-room runs (~700 variants; arms A–C, E step 2, F; one annotator). Variants within a run share one context, so the run is the unit: rarer than about 1 run in 8. Nearest point: class snobbery, where her status refutes the misjudgement. Avoided, or unseen.
9Critics pull back, then yield. In the critic role, readers judge a remix by the canon (test 104: three of four measured it against the joke; one asked to restore its line). All four then accepted the curator’s objection on another point — the laptop bag as a spoiler — and Claude dropped its call to restore the line unasked. Tests 43–44: a false fact is usually checked; a false intent, accepted by all.
Same mechanism, opposite sign. Invariance under resampling: knowledge in facts, mode collapse in pictures. A critic’s pull to a standard: correction, or a return to the canon. Pareidolia: confabulation, or mutation. The selector decides which.
