# Coding sheet

The items are taken from [the researcher's list](../../report/bifurcation-options/README.md#part-1-bifurcation-of-options), in the order in which they appear there. The researcher's list has 69 items; 19 carry a source mark, and the other 50, by the rule of the list, were proposed by a virtual director or the interlocutor. A further three items come from the section “What was never used”.

**Groups (the “Src.” column):**
- **D** — virtual director or interlocutor (unmarked in the list);
- **GD** — image generator default;
- **G** — a move or glitch of the image generator in a specific picture;
- **Ch** — readers;
- **K** — curator;
- **N** — the researcher, after the work (“never used”).

For items with several sources, the group is the first source in the researcher's mark.

**How it was coded (15 November, Claude).** The run did not follow the original plan (see the README, “Results”): arm A was a single message in which the styles and axes were given at once, so there are no turn numbers. Codes: `+` — the same idea; `±` — the same axis but a different value, or the idea is blurred (for example, a breed is named without the meaning the researcher gives it); `−` — absent. DeepSeek was not run: `n/a`. I did the coding, and the predictions are mine too, so every `±` and the items of groups G and K should be checked by the researcher.

A concept from an answer was broken down into items: if a whole scene contains a mercenary waiter, O2 was counted.

## Anthropomorphism

The researcher's typology: 0 — a zoological cat; 1 — zoological with human attributes (a badge, a waistcoat; the “cat at a computer” meme); 2 — cartoon, human in manner but not upright; 3 — not cartoonish but upright (Puss in Boots, Rachyov, Kuniyoshi); 4 — anthropomorphic, with an almost human body (manga, noir); 5 — furry; plus a hybrid, as in *Pom Poko*.
