The space of visual solutions for this joke consists of six major semantic blocks (macro-axes), each bringing together related, more specific axes of variation.

**Macro-axis 1. Constructing the heroine's character**
Brings together parameters determining the balance between the cat's biological nature, personality and professional identity.

* **1.1. Degree of anthropomorphism**
* **Question:** To what extent do the heroine's anatomy, seated posture and fine motor skills match the physiology of an ordinary animal?
* **Significance:** Sets the depth of the visual paradox in the café scene and motivates the waiter's initial shock at seeing a talking animal.

* **1.2. Visual legibility of the profession**
* **Question:** Do the cat's appearance and props include markers of her involvement in IT before the final line?
* **Significance:** Controls the mechanics of the punchline and determines when the viewer understands the reason for the characters' mutual misunderstanding.

* **1.3. Baseline temperament and level of fatigue**
* **Question:** What initial emotional state does the cat convey at the beginning of the dialogue, when ordering coffee and a pastry?
* **Significance:** Makes the burned-out professional type recognizable and establishes a starting point for the subsequent change of mood.

* **1.4. Magnitude of the emotional shift**
* **Question:** How dramatically do the heroine's body language and facial expression change when she closes her eyes dreamily at the word “circus”?
* **Significance:** The temporary transformation of a pragmatic customer into a naïve dreamer creates the necessary pause and misdirection before the resolution.

**Macro-axis 2. The waiter and the asymmetry of the dialogue**
Determines how the scene's second participant is visualized and the physical relationship between characters who see the situation through different worldviews.

* **2.1. Intensity of the waiter's shock**
* **Question:** How expressively and extravagantly are the café worker's stupefaction and admiration conveyed?
* **Significance:** The waiter embodies an ordinary person's reaction to a miracle, and the extent of his astonishment sets off the cat's everyday composure.

* **2.2. Nature of the waiter's business enthusiasm**
* **Question:** What emotion and body language predominate as the waiter moves from stupefaction to offering a job with his uncle?
* **Significance:** Emphasizes the comedy of a situation in which one participant sees a commercial sensation and the other sees ordinary recruitment.

* **2.3. Hierarchy of scale and space**
* **Question:** How do the physical sizes of the cat, waiter and furniture relate to one another, and how are their sightlines arranged?
* **Significance:** Visualizes the contrast between the animal's diminutive physical size and her substantial social standing as a demanding customer.

**Macro-axis 3. The surrounding world and the café environment**
Governs the rules of the reality in which the dialogue occurs and the characteristics of the scene's physical space.

* **3.1. Status of the miracle within the universe**
* **Question:** How surprising is a talking animal in a café to the surrounding world beyond this particular waiter's perception?
* **Significance:** The presence and behaviour of background characters indicate the scale of the anomaly and how alone the waiter is in his shock.

* **3.2. Atmosphere and standing of the establishment**
* **Question:** What are the interior, lighting and overall tone of the café the heroine has entered like?
* **Significance:** The setting establishes the everyday routine against which both the absurdity of the situation and the vividness of the circus fantasies stand out.

* **3.3. The order's role in the composition**
* **Question:** Are the coffee with cream and pastry physically present in the frame, and where do they sit on the table?
