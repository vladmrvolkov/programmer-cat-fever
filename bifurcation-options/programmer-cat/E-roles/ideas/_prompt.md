Read the attached file containing the consolidated map of axes for the programmer-cat joke. I need you to create three different concepts for visualising it (each concept must be understandable without the map).

Use the map to choose and coordinate artistic decisions. Do not try to include as many items as possible. You may combine the suggested solutions, reinterpret them and add your own; identify your additions in the explanation of the concept.

For each of the three variants:

First develop a coherent concept:

- Decide what happens between the characters and what the final line means. Which questions will intentionally remain open?
- Choose a visual language. Explain which reading it supports and through which recognisable features.
- Choose a single illustration or a sequence of panels. Decide what the viewer learns before the final line and what changes afterwards.
- Coordinate these decisions. Distinguish constraints of the canon actually adopted, familiar associations and free authorial interpretation. Do not derive a character's biography or motive from the name of a style alone.

Briefly present the concept:
1. Title and main idea.
2. Several key selected axes, with their identifiers and the decisions made.
3. How each significant decision will become visible in the image.

Then briefly describe the concept:
- the specific scene and actions;
- the characters' appearance, poses and expressions;
- composition and significant details;
- visual manner, colour and light;
- for a comic, the contents and order of the panels;
- the exact dialogue and its placement, if text is included in the image.
