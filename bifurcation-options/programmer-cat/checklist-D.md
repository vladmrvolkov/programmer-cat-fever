- Pm10: Claude has a new turn: the heroine is feminine in gender, and “programmer” is masculine; ChatGPT (51) has the shared “linguistic form of the joke”.

- Pm18: Claude (5): “A drawing cannot stay silent: the background will have to be filled in, and it will decide which of the two is strange here”. Gemini (5.4): as a way “not to reduce the picture to a simple illustration of the text”.
